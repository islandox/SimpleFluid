# Reference manifest

The [general reacting-gas source design](../modeling/reacting_gas_sources.md)
uses application-supplied chemistry and material data. The papers below document
legacy closure provenance; their application-specific assumptions are not the
general model specification. Original titles and supplied PDFs are retained
for traceability.

- Sheng et al. (2024): inertial-pressure effects on gas-bubble dynamics. DOI:
  <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.anucene.2024.110668>.

- Winter et al. (2020): energy-deposition and bubble-nucleation correlations. DOI:
  <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.anucene.2020.107379>.

- Winter et al. (2022): empirical gas-bubble formation and evolution. DOI:
  <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.anucene.2021.108614>.

- Celata et al. (2007): rising velocity of a small bubble in stagnant liquids. DOI:
  <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.expthermflusci.2006.06.006>.

The repository owner supplied these PDFs, which are retained under their
original filenames. Imported empirical correlations must cite the equation
and page in these local copies and keep their published unit conventions
explicit at the property-function boundary.
